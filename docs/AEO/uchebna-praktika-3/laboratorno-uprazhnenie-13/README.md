# Laboratory exercise 13

## Presentation and Discussion of the Developed Solutions

### Objective

The objective of this exercise is to present and discuss the solutions developed during the independent work in Laboratory Exercises 11 and 12 and to analyze the use of the AI assistant throughout the development process.

### Presentation and Discussion

During the exercise, the developed application should be presented and the core business process should be demonstrated.

The presentation should include a discussion of:

- the chosen model and application structure;
- the implementation of the main business rules;
- the migration from H2 to PostgreSQL;
- the interaction between the backend and the user interface;
- the selected approaches to automated testing;
- how the AI assistant was used;
- AI assistant suggestions that were accepted, corrected, or rejected, together with the reasons for those decisions.

Different solutions to the same problem may be compared during the discussion, including an analysis of their advantages and disadvantages.

### Result

By the end of the exercise, students should be able to justify the main decisions made during development and evaluate the role of the AI assistant in the decision-making process.
