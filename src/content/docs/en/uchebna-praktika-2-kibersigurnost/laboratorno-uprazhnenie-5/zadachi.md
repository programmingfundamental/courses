## Task 1

Check a numeric palindrome such as 12321 entered as text.

## Task 2

Instead of skipping punctuation, first normalize the string, then compare the normalized text with its reversed version.

## Task 3

Check whether the text is a palindrome using exact character comparison, without ignoring letter case or punctuation.

## Task 4

Check whether each individual element of the line, separated by spaces, is a palindrome.

## Task 5

Check for a palindrome while ignoring only spaces, but preserving punctuation and letter case.

## Task 6

Find the longest prefix of the text that is a palindrome.

## Task 7

Display the first pair of positions that do not match during the palindrome check.

## Task 8

Check whether the entered text is a numeric palindrome and reject negative numbers.

## Task 9

Compare the two-index palindrome check with a check performed by reversing the string.

## Task 10

Check whether the text can become a palindrome by removing at most one character.
