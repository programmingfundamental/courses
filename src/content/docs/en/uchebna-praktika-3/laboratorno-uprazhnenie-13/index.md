# Laboratory Exercise 13

## Presentation and Discussion of the Developed Solutions

### Objective

The objective of this exercise is to present and discuss the solutions developed during the independent work in Laboratory Exercises 11 and 12 and to analyze the use of the AI assistant during the development process.

The main focus is on the strategy used for working with AI, its adaptation during development, the methods used to verify the obtained results, and the role of the developer in making the final decisions.

The developed application and the AI_WORKLOG.md file maintained throughout Laboratory Exercises 11 and 12 are used as the basis for the presentation and discussion.
