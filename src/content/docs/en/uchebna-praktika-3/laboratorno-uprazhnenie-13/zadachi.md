### Presentation of the Developed Application

Demonstrate the developed application through the main business process.

The demonstration should include:

•	a successful scenario of the main business process; 

•	a case in which an operation is rejected because of a violated business rule; 

•	interaction between the user interface and the backend application; 

•	execution of selected automated tests. 

If the additional task from Laboratory Exercise 12 involving migration to PostgreSQL has been completed, the application running with PostgreSQL may also be demonstrated.

Briefly present the main decisions related to the application model and structure, the implementation of the business rules, the interaction between the frontend and backend, and the selected approach to automated testing.

The objective is not to present every implemented class or component in detail, but to demonstrate the working solution and the main engineering decisions on which it is based.

### Presentation and Discussion of the Engineering Process

For the presentation, select specific examples from AI_WORKLOG.md that demonstrate significant moments in the work with the AI assistant.

It is not necessary to present all documented interactions. The selected examples should make it possible to explain the decisions made and the evolution of the strategy during development.

The presentation should cover:

•	the initially selected strategy for working with AI; 

•	an example of selecting or changing the project context according to a specific task; 

•	a case in which the initial approach was changed because of an obtained result or an encountered problem; 

•	an AI proposal or generated solution that was accepted, corrected, or rejected; 

•	the way in which a specific proposal or generated solution was independently verified. 

For the selected cases, it should be possible to explain **why the corresponding decision was made and what verification it was based on**.

When presenting a problem that occurred, it should be possible to explain how its cause was identified and how this affected the subsequent work with the AI.

### Discussion of Different Approaches

During the discussion, different solutions and strategies used when working on the same business case may be compared.

The discussion may analyze how the process and the resulting solution were influenced by:

•	the choice of context; 

•	the decomposition and sequence of tasks; 

•	the scope of the proposed changes; 

•	the method used to verify the results; 

•	the approach taken when problems occurred and were analyzed. 

Differences between the developed solutions should not automatically be treated as errors. They should be evaluated against the given business case, the assumptions made, the state of the project, and the verification that was performed.

The objective of the comparison is not to determine a single universal strategy for working with AI, but to discuss how appropriate the selected approach was for the specific task and how it was adapted according to the results obtained.

### Result

At the end of the exercise, it should be possible to justify:

•	the main engineering decisions made during the development of the application; 

•	the strategy used for interacting with AI and the significant changes made to it; 

•	the selection and modification of the required context; 

•	the methods used to verify proposals and generated solutions; 

•	the decisions to accept, correct, or reject AI proposals. 

The evaluation of the engineering process is not based on the number of prompts used, the amount of generated code, or the number of documented interactions, but on the ability to justify the selected approach, the context used, the verification performed, and the decisions made.

AI_WORKLOG.md serves as evidence and support for the discussion of the process, but it does not replace the developer's presentation and justification.
