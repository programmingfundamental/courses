# Лабораторно упражнение 11

## Independent Development of a Strategy for Working with AI

### Objective

The objective of this exercise is to independently develop and apply a strategy for working with an AI assistant when developing a new software project.

Based on a given business case, the necessary stages of work should be determined, appropriate context should be selected, tasks for the AI should be formulated, and the resulting proposals and generated solutions should be evaluated and verified.

The sequence of interaction with the AI, the required context, and the approach to refining, correcting, and verifying the results are determined independently according to the current task and the state of the project.

During development, key engineering decisions and significant changes in the strategy for working with AI should be documented. The documentation will be used during the final presentation and discussion of the developed solution.
