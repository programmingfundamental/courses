This course introduces the fundamentals of Kotlin and Android application development with Jetpack Compose. You will study user interfaces, state management, event handling, and asynchronous work with coroutines.

## Labs

- [Lab 1 — Introduction to Kotlin — syntax, types, operators, and null safety](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-1/)
- [Lab 2 — Classes, objects, properties, constructors, and inheritance](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-2/)
- [Lab 3 — Test on Kotlin fundamentals](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-3/)
- [Lab 4 — Android Studio, the Android SDK, the emulator, and Compose project structure](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-4/)
- [Lab 5 — Building user interfaces with Jetpack Compose](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-5/)
- [Lab 6 — Activity lifecycle, composition lifecycle, and a reactive approach](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-6/)
- [Lab 7 — Forms, input, validation, state, and user events](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-7/)
- [Lab 8 — Test on forms, calculations, and state](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-8/)
- [Lab 9 — Lists and grids: LazyColumn, LazyRow, and lazy grids](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-9/)
- [Lab 10 — Coroutines, suspend, Dispatchers, and structured concurrency](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-10/)
- [Lab 11 — Review exercise: asynchronous catalog loading](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-11/)
- [Lab 12 — Review exercise: personal study planner](/courses/en/programirane-za-mobilni-i-internet-ustroistva-kotlin/laboratorno-uprazhnenie-12/)
